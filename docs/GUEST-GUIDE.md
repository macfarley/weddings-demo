# Guest Guide — Wedding Photo Experience

Welcome to the John & Crystal wedding site! Here's everything you need to know
about sharing photos, signing the guestbook, and accessing the gallery.

---

## Sharing Photos

### How to upload a photo

1. Go to **Send Your Photos** (linked in the main menu, or scan the QR code at your table).
2. Enter your name (optional) and your family name or group nickname.
3. Write a short caption for your photo.
4. Tap or click the file picker and choose a photo from your camera roll or computer.
5. Tap **Submit**.

Your photo will be reviewed before it appears in the gallery. This usually takes
a few hours.

### Photo guidelines

- **Supported formats:** JPEG, PNG, HEIC
- **Maximum size:** 5 MB per photo
- **One photo per submission** — submit again for more
- Keep it kind and celebratory — photos with inappropriate content will not be approved

### Why isn't my photo in the gallery yet?

Photos are reviewed by a real person before they go live. This keeps the gallery
looking its best and ensures all guests feel comfortable. We aim to approve
photos within a few hours. If you submitted during the reception, expect to see
your photo the following morning.

---

## The Guestbook

### How to leave a message

1. Go to the **Guestbook** page from the main menu.
2. Fill in your name and message.
3. Hit **Sign the Guestbook**.

Messages are reviewed before they appear publicly. Approved messages are visible
to all site visitors.

---

## The Gallery

The gallery shows all approved guest photos. You can:

- **Sort** photos by newest or oldest using the sort menu.
- **Tap any photo** to view it full-size.
- **Download** a photo by opening it full-size and using the download button.
- **React** with a ❤ to show your appreciation for a photo.

---

## Your Privacy

### What information is collected

When you submit a photo or guestbook entry, we collect:

- Your name and family name (if provided — both are optional)
- Your caption or message
- Your photo file
- The date and time of your submission

We do **not** collect email addresses, phone numbers, or account information.
We do **not** use cookies for tracking. We do **not** share your data with
advertising networks.

### Where your data is stored

Photos and messages are stored in a secure cloud database operated by Supabase
(hosted in the United States). The gallery is served through Cloudflare.

### How long data is kept

Photos and guestbook entries submitted for this event will be kept for as long
as the couple wishes to preserve them, and then deleted.

### Requesting removal

If you want your photo or guestbook message removed, contact the couple directly
or reach out using the **Contact** page on this site. We will remove your
content promptly, no questions asked.

---

## Downloading Your Photos

You can download any photo from the gallery:

1. Open the photo in the full-size viewer.
2. Tap the **Download** button (↓ icon) in the viewer.

The original-quality file will be saved to your device.

---

## Need Help?

If you run into any issues — photo upload not working, gallery not loading, etc. —
try refreshing the page. If problems persist, use the **Contact** page to let us know.
